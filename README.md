In this moment this is more like a demo but you're a simple happy man fighting carrots that somehow came alive as you press space to attack in the direction you were facing you notice, as they die, they drop a carrot. You open up you're inventory with tab and click the slot with the carrot, you press q and voila, you're health is a little healed and its gone from you're inventory. Also you lose hearts when touching the bad carrot. WASD is to move and arrow keys, r is to reset, space to attack, q to use, tab to open inventory. Also my use of ai was to detect bugs and help me with some functionalities of godot like connecting signals!
